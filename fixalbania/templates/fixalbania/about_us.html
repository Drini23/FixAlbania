From a small workshop to a trusted name

FixAlbania started in 2019 as a one-man repair shop in the heart of Tirana. Founder Erjon B. saw that locals often threw away repairable electronics because they couldn't find reliable service. Today, we’re a team of 28 professionals, serving thousands each month — and still growing. Our motto: "Nëse mund të rregullohet, ne mund ta rregullojmë." (If it can be fixed, we can fix it.)
